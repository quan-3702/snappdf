SnapPDF – How to use

Turn photos and scans into one tidy PDF file. This guide walks through every button.

1. Make your first PDF

  1. Open SnapPDF.
  2. Drag your pictures onto the big box that says Drop images here, or click the box and choose them. You can also copy a picture and press Ctrl+V.
  3. Each picture becomes one page. Check the order of the pages.
  4. Type a name in File name if you like.
  5. Click Create PDF. The PDF is saved in your Downloads folder.

SnapPDF accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP pictures. Photos from a phone or a scanner both work.

2. Put the pages in the right order

  • Drag a page with the mouse to move it.
  • Sort A–Z arranges the pages by file name – handy for scans named 001, 002, 003.
  • Reverse turns the whole order around.
  • Click ⟳ on a page to rotate it when a photo lies on its side.
  • Click ✕ on a page to remove it, or Clear all to start again.

3. Choose how the pages look

  • Page size: A4, US Letter, US Legal or A5. Same as image makes every page exactly as large as its picture.
  • Orientation: Auto picks upright or sideways for each picture. You can also force Portrait or Landscape.
  • Margin: None, Small or Large white border around each picture.
  • Color: Original colors, or Grayscale to save ink.
  • Image quality: move the slider left for a smaller file, right for a sharper one. 85 is a good everyday value.

4. Make a small file for e-mail or online forms

Many websites only accept files under 1 or 2 MB.

  1. Under PDF size choose a limit, for example Under 1 MB. (Pro)
  2. Click Create PDF. SnapPDF lowers the picture quality step by step until the file fits.

Without Pro you can still make the file smaller by moving Image quality to the left and choosing Grayscale.

5. Scan documents with your phone

  1. Photograph each sheet from straight above, in good light.
  2. Copy the photos to your PC and add them to SnapPDF.
  3. Under Color choose Scan document (B&W). (Pro) Grey paper and shadows turn into a clean white page with black text.

6. Watermark, page numbers and password (Pro)

  • Watermark: type a word such as COPY or your name. It is printed lightly across every page.
  • Page numbers: adds 1, 2, 3 … at the bottom of the pages.
  • Password: type a password. The PDF then asks for it every time it is opened. Write the password down – it cannot be recovered.

7. Free version and Pro

The free version makes PDFs with up to 10 pages and includes all layout settings. Pro is a one-time purchase and adds unlimited pages, compress-to-size, scan mode, watermark, page numbers and password. Click ★ Get Pro at the top to see the price. If you bought Pro before, click Restore purchase there.

8. If something goes wrong

  • A picture is not added: it may be in a format SnapPDF cannot read (for example HEIC from an iPhone). Convert it to JPG first.
  • The PDF is too large: lower Image quality or choose Grayscale.
  • I cannot find my PDF: open File Explorer and look in Downloads. The newest file is at the top when you sort by date.
